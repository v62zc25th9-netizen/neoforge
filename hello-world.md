NeoForge Hello Cart

Goal:
Boot an original AES with a NeoForge-authored program.

Requirements:
- AES-compatible cartridge connector
- Minimal valid cartridge header
- 68K P ROM
- Required cartridge decoding
- No SD
- No menu
- No multicart
- No FPGA initially
- No copyrighted game ROMs
